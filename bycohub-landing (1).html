Questions, answered.

Can't find yours? Send it through the contact form and we'll help.

What is BYCOHUB?

A connected motorcycle platform. When your bike breaks down, you describe the problem, pick a nearby mechanic and track them to your location. Over time it grows into one app for your bike, your riders and your everyday rides.

When does the app launch?

Soon. Join the waitlist and we'll message you first when access opens.

Where will it be available first?

Launch areas will be announced to waitlist members first.

How does pricing work?

You see the service, the expected time and the price before you confirm your request.

Can I cancel a request?

Yes. Your active request has a cancel option, and you can message your mechanic from the same screen.

How do I pay?

Payments happen securely inside the app. You confirm your payment method before the mechanic is dispatched.

I'm a mechanic. Can I join?

Send us a message through the contact form and tell us where you work and what you repair.
